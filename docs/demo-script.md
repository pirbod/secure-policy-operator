# Demo Script

## 1. Start With The Business Problem

Explain that bespoke customer cloud delivery slows sales, onboarding, operations, and support. The factory approach replaces one-off work with reusable patterns.

## 2. Show Executive Overview

Open the dashboard and highlight customer environments, readiness, policy compliance, onboarding time reduction, support self-service potential, incident reduction, and multi-cloud coverage.

## 3. Show Pre-Sales Assistant

Select a mock customer and generate the pre-sales pack. Emphasize clear technical answers for platform fit, prerequisites, timeline, observability, policy, support model, and data dependencies.

## 4. Generate Customer Blueprint

Open Deployment Blueprints, choose a customer and platform, then generate YAML. Point out Terraform modules, GitOps repo layout, policy pack, observability pack, CI/CD gates, runbooks, and go-live plan.

## 5. Run Readiness Assessment

Open Onboarding Factory, run readiness assessment, and show prerequisites, missing information, blockers, automatable steps, and support handover requirements.

## 6. Show Policy Operator

Evaluate policies and explain the flow: Git, CI policy validation, ArgoCD PreSync, Gatekeeper or cloud policy, runtime audit, and support runbook.

## 7. Simulate Support Case

Open Support Runbooks, select a known case, triage it, and show the recommended runbook and engineering involvement flag.

## 8. Recommend Runbook

Click Recommend Runbook and show confidence, runbook steps, and expected outcome.

## 9. Close With Business Outcomes

Close on measurable outcomes: onboarding reduced from 12 weeks to 5 days, 65 percent deployment automation, 40 percent fewer consistency incidents, 55 percent support self-service potential, 80 percent policy coverage target, and 90 percent observability target.
